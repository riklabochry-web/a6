Cookie Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

1. What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small data files stored by a browser. Similar technologies, including local storage, can remember a preference or help measure how a website is used. They do not give us direct access to other files on your device.

2. Essential preferences

We may store a simple consent choice so the privacy notice does not appear on every page. This preference supports requested site behaviour and does not create an advertising profile.

3. Optional analytics

Google Analytics is configured with analytics storage denied by default. If you choose Accept, measurement may collect information such as visited pages, approximate device characteristics and interaction timing. We use aggregated patterns to improve content and navigation, not to make decisions about individual readers.

4. No advertising cookies

Precision Watch Archive does not intentionally deploy behavioural advertising cookies or sell browsing data. A third-party page reached through an external link may use its own technologies under separate terms.

5. Managing your choice

You can reject optional analytics in the consent notice. You can also clear site data, block cookies or restrict tracking in browser settings. Blocking essential storage may cause the notice to reappear but core editorial pages should remain readable.

6. Duration and changes

Browser-stored preferences persist until removed or replaced. Analytics retention is governed by the configured service settings and applicable safeguards. We may update this policy when technologies or legal requirements change.

7. Contact

Questions about cookies may be sent to editorial@precisionwatcharchive.com. You can also write to Precision Watch Archive, 47 Meridian Workshop, Chicago, IL 60611, United States, or call +1-888-442-9076.
