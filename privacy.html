Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

1. Scope of this policy

This policy explains how Precision Watch Archive handles information when you browse our editorial pages, use collection filters, manage analytics preferences or contact our editorial desk. It applies only to this website and not to third-party sites that may be linked from an article.

2. Information you provide

If you submit the contact form, we may receive your name, email address and message. Please do not include payment details, government identifiers, health information or other sensitive data. We use submitted information only to respond, keep an appropriate record of the conversation and protect the site from misuse.

3. Technical and analytics information

Basic server records may include an IP address, browser type, requested page, time of access and security events. Optional Google Analytics measurement remains denied unless you select Accept in the consent notice. When consent is granted, aggregated usage information can help us understand popular guides and improve navigation.

4. Legal bases and purposes

Depending on your location, processing may rely on consent, our legitimate interest in operating a secure editorial website, or the need to respond to a request you initiate. We do not sell personal information, build advertising profiles or use contact details for unrelated marketing.

5. Retention and protection

Enquiry records are kept only as long as reasonably required for follow-up, record keeping and legal obligations. We apply proportionate organisational and technical safeguards, but no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Service providers and international use

Hosting, security and analytics providers may process limited data on our behalf under their own safeguards. Because online services can operate internationally, information may be handled outside your region where permitted by applicable law.

7. Your rights and choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete information connected with an enquiry, object to certain processing or withdraw analytics consent. Browser controls can also remove stored preferences. Rights vary by jurisdiction and may be subject to lawful exceptions.

8. Children and external links

This general-interest journal is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. External websites have separate privacy practices; review those policies before providing information.

9. Updates and contact

Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised date. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to editorial@precisionwatcharchive.com or Precision Watch Archive, 47 Meridian Workshop, Chicago, IL 60611, United States. Phone: +1-888-442-9076.
